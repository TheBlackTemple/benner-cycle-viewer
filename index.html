The Benner Cycle

An Ohio farmer's 1875 forecast of panics, good times, and hard times, reconstructed from his exact formulas and set beside the long waves economists charted afterward.

▲ Good times — high prices, sell Hard times — low prices, buy ▼

The chart is two of Benner's cycles layered together, as in his original: the bold major cycle runs Panic to Panic on his 20-16-18 year formula, and the lighter minor cycle runs Good Times to Hard Times on his 8-9-10 / 9-7-11 year formula — both reconstructed exactly from his published tables through 2059. The Kondratiev, Kitchin, and Juglar overlays are illustrative sine curves for comparison only — unlike Benner's fixed, falsifiable table, their real-world dating and even their existence are disputed among economists.
